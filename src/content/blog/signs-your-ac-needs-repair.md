Pennsylvania summers can be brutal. Catching AC problems early saves money and keeps your home comfortable.

## 1. Warm Air from the Vents

If the system runs but the air isn't cool, you may have low refrigerant, a failing compressor, or a clogged coil. Schedule a diagnostic promptly.

## 2. Unusual Noises

Grinding, squealing, or rattling often means a motor bearing, loose fan, or debris. Ignoring noise usually leads to a larger failure.

## 3. Short Cycling

When the unit turns on and off rapidly, it wastes energy and stresses components. Common causes include thermostat issues, oversized equipment, or refrigerant problems.

## 4. Higher Than Normal Energy Bills

A sudden spike with no change in usage often points to reduced efficiency — dirty filters, dirty coils, or a system working harder than it should.

## 5. Water Leaks Around the Indoor Unit

Clogged condensate drains are common in humid weather. Left alone they can cause water damage and mold growth.

If you notice any of these signs in your Newtown Square home, call for a same-day inspection. Early repair almost always costs less than emergency replacement.
