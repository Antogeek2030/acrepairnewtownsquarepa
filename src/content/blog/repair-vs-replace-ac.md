When the estimate arrives, the repair-vs-replace question is common. Here is a practical framework we share with homeowners.

## Repair Usually Makes Sense When
- The system is under 10–12 years old
- The repair is under roughly half the cost of a comparable new unit
- The problem is isolated (capacitor, contactor, fan motor, etc.)
- The system has been reliable until now

## Consider Replacement When
- The unit is 12–15+ years old
- You face recurring failures or multiple major components
- Refrigerant is an older type (R-22) that is expensive or phased out
- Energy bills have climbed significantly
- You want better humidity control or quieter operation

We always present both options with clear numbers. No pressure — just the information you need to decide.
