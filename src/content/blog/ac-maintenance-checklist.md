Regular maintenance is the cheapest way to avoid mid-summer breakdowns.

## Monthly / As Needed
- Replace or clean air filters
- Keep outdoor unit free of leaves, grass, and debris
- Ensure supply and return vents are open and unobstructed

## Spring Tune-Up (Recommended)
- Professional coil cleaning (indoor and outdoor)
- Refrigerant level check
- Electrical connection inspection and tightening
- Capacitor and contactor testing
- Thermostat calibration
- Condensate drain flush

## Why It Matters Locally
Newtown Square and Delaware County see high humidity and pollen. Dirty coils and clogged drains are among the most common service calls we handle. A yearly professional tune-up typically pays for itself in lower energy use and fewer emergency repairs.
